Dashcam Studio — how it works

Open folder loads every clip in recording order, matches each front clip with its rear (and TriPro third-camera) partners, and sorts them by day and type (driving, locked after a bump or the emergency button, and parked). Clips that are damaged, usually from a power cut mid-recording, are labelled instead of playing blank.

Keeping clips: click the ☆ beside a clip (or press K) to keep it. Kept clips stay starred next time you open the folder. The ★ Kept tab lists them from every day: use Add all to edit to put them on the timeline, or Copy originals to copy the untouched files into a "Kept clips" folder inside your save folder, safe from the dashcam's loop recording.

Merging and cutting: clips recorded back to back are grouped into drives in the list. Click Merge on a drive to lay all of its clips end to end in the edit timeline. Click anywhere on the timeline to jump there, then press C to cut at the playhead. Select a piece you don't want and press ⌫ to remove it. Or mark in (I) and out (O) around the unwanted part and press X to cut it out. Play edit plays the result, skipping what you removed. ⌘Z undoes.

Picking sections: you can also build an edit the other way round: mark the start (I) and end (O) of what you want to keep and add it (A). Drag pieces to reorder them.

Export copies the original video without re-compressing it, so it's fast and full quality. Section starts snap back to the nearest keyframe (usually less than a second earlier); ends are exact. GPS is kept in exported files, so they reopen here with their route.

Save folder: in Chrome, choose a folder once and every export goes straight into it. Other browsers save to Downloads. From there, drag the files into Photos or iMovie.
