Dashcam Studio — how it works

Open folder loads every clip in recording order, matches each front clip with its rear (and TriPro third-camera) partners, and sorts them by day and type (driving, locked after a bump or the emergency button, and parked). Clips that are damaged, usually from a power cut mid-recording, are labelled instead of playing blank.

Keeping clips: click the ☆ beside a clip (or press K) to keep it. Kept clips stay starred next time you open the folder. The ★ Kept tab lists them from every day: use Add all to edit to put them on the timeline, or Copy originals to copy the untouched files into a "Kept clips" folder inside your save folder, safe from the dashcam's loop recording.

Finding the moments: each day plays as one continuous video. The strip at the bottom covers the whole day: drag along it to move through the video, click to jump, hover to preview, or click the route on the map. The blue line under it is your speed and the shading is your height, so slow crawls, stops and the highest point are easy to spot. Play at 4× or 8× to skim.

Saving a moment: press Mark start where it begins and Mark end where it ends, then ⭐ Save as a moment. A moment can run across several 3-minute files. Give it a name on its card. Each moment can be saved as its own video, and the ones ticked "In highlight reel" join into one video with Save highlight reel. Moments are remembered in this browser, and come back when you open the same folder again.

Saving videos copies the original video without re-compressing it, so it's fast and full quality. Section starts snap back to the nearest keyframe (usually less than a second earlier); ends are exact. GPS is kept in exported files, so they reopen here with their route.

Save folder: in Chrome, choose a folder once and every export goes straight into it. Other browsers save to Downloads. From there, drag the files into Photos or iMovie.
